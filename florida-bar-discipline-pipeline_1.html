CH. 3 · RULES REGULATING THE FLORIDA BAR

Anatomy of a Bar Discipline Case

Five stages carry every case from open to close. A sixth exists on paper but almost never convenes. One office is in the room for all of them.

    FINAL

    Trace the file start to finish and one office never leaves the room: bar counsel screens the inquiry, builds the grievance committee's case, drafts the formal complaint, and tries it before the referee. The 52-member Board of Governors touches the file at exactly one point — through a single assigned reviewer who is free to do nothing at all, and usually does. The real check on the Bar's judgment isn't governance. It's adjudication: a referee, and then the Supreme Court itself.
